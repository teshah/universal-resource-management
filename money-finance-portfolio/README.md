# Money, Finance & Portfolio Management

A structured domain module applying the **Universal Resource Management** framework to wealth management, capital allocation, and retirement portfolio engineering.

## Overview

In the developmental hierarchy of human effort, **Money is Currency #5 (The External Multiplier)**. 
When disconnected from foundational human currencies (Energy, Time, Willpower, and Skill), financial management often degenerates into myopic, high-stress speculation.

This module analyzes how to engineer a balance sheet that maximizes **lifetime utility, tax efficiency, and peace of mind** rather than chasing volatile, short-term market indices.
